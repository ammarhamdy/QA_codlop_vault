# M12 — Marketing & Sales

## Overview / Module Purpose

This module manages sales activities, marketing campaigns, customer acquisition, conversion tracking, campaign attribution, and marketing performance measurement.

---

## Scope

This module covers:

* Sales management.
* Sales representatives.
* Representative codes.
* Membership sales.
* Medical service sales.
* Marketing campaigns.
* Campaign objectives and budgets.
* Campaign channels.
* Campaign links.
* UTM tracking.
* Referral tracking.
* Coupon and promotional codes.
* Conversion tracking.
* Campaign attribution.
* Customer acquisition tracking.
* Marketing metrics.
* Sales performance.
* Campaign performance.
* Revenue attribution.
* Cost and conversion analysis.
