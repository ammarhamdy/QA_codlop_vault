# M09 — Content & Discovery

## Overview / Module Purpose

This module manages promotional and discovery content that helps customers discover medical centers, services, offers, and related content.

---

## Scope

This module covers:

* Discover content.
* Reels / short videos.
* Promotional content.
* Content visibility.
* Content ordering.
* Content publishing.
* Content scheduling.
* Content engagement.
* Views.
* Shares.
* Favorites.
* Links to centers, offers, and services.
