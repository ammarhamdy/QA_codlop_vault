# Preconditions
- The Contact Us page is accessible.

# Steps
1. Navigate to the Contact Us page.
2. Locate the contact information section.
3. Verify that the organization's email address is visible.
4. Verify that the organization's phone number is visible.
5. Verify that the organization's WhatsApp number is visible.
6. Verify that the organization's physical address is visible.

# Expected Result
- All specified contact information (email, phone, WhatsApp, address) is clearly visible and accurately displayed on the Contact Us page.
