# Preconditions
- The website's home page is accessible.
- Partner logos are configured.

# Steps
1. Navigate to the Home Page.
2. Scroll down to the Partners section.
3. Verify the presence and correctness of the section title and description.
4. Verify that all 10 partner logos are visible.
5. Check each logo for visual correctness (no distortion, no truncation).

# Expected Result
- Section title and description match the configured values.
- 10 distinct partner logos are displayed in the section.
- All logos are displayed clearly without any distortion or truncation.
