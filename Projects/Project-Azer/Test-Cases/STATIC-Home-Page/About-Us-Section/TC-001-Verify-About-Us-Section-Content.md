# Preconditions
- The website's home page is accessible.

# Steps
1. Navigate to the Home Page.
2. Scroll down to the About Us section.
3. Verify the presence and correctness of the section title.
4. Verify the presence and correctness of the section description.
5. Verify the presence of the section image.

# Expected Result
- The About Us title is visible and matches the configured content.
- The About Us description is visible and matches the configured content.
- The About Us image is visible and loads correctly.
