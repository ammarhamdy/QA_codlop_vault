# Story Description

**As a Customer**  
**I want to** view product details, navigate to the product category, add products to my cart or favorites, adjust the quantity, ask questions, rate products, and browse recommended products  
**So that** I can make an informed purchase decision and interact with products easily.

## Acceptance Criteria

### **Product Details**

- [ ] **Scenario 1: View Product Details**
  - **Given** I select a product
  - **When** the Product Details page loads
  - **Then** the product information should be displayed correctly.

- [ ] **Scenario 2: Navigate to the Product Category**
  - **Given** I am viewing the Product Details page
  - **When** I tap the category link
  - **Then** I should be redirected to the corresponding category page.

- [ ] **Scenario 3: Add Product to Favorites**
  - **Given** I am viewing a product
  - **When** I tap the Favorite icon
  - **Then** the product should be added to my Favorites successfully.

- [ ] **Scenario 4: Remove Product from Favorites**
  - **Given** the product is already in my Favorites
  - **When** I tap the Favorite icon again
  - **Then** the product should be removed from my Favorites successfully.

- [ ] **Scenario 5: Add Product to Cart**
  - **Given** I am viewing a product
  - **When** I tap the Add to Cart button
  - **Then** the product should be added to my cart successfully.

- [ ] **Scenario 6: Update Product Quantity**
  - **Given** I am viewing a product
  - **When** I increase or decrease the quantity
  - **Then** the selected quantity should be updated correctly.

- [ ] **Scenario 7: Submit a Product Question**
  - **Given** I am viewing a product
  - **When** I submit a question about the product
  - **Then** the question should be submitted successfully.

- [ ] **Scenario 8: Rate the Product**
  - **Given** I am viewing a product
  - **When** I submit a rating
  - **Then** my rating should be saved successfully.

### **Recommended Products**

- [ ] **Scenario 9: View Recommended Products**
  - **Given** I am viewing the Product Details page
  - **When** the page loads
  - **Then** the recommended products section should be displayed.

- [ ] **Scenario 10: Open Recommended Product Details**
  - **Given** the recommended products section is displayed
  - **When** I tap a recommended product
  - **Then** I should be redirected to the selected Product Details page.

- [ ] **Scenario 11: Add Recommended Product to Favorites**
  - **Given** the recommended products section is displayed
  - **When** I tap the Favorite icon on a recommended product
  - **Then** the product should be added to my Favorites successfully.

- [ ] **Scenario 12: Add Recommended Product to Cart**
  - **Given** the recommended products section is displayed
  - **When** I tap the Add to Cart button on a recommended product
  - **Then** the product should be added to my cart successfully.
