# Real Estate Management System – Overview

## 1. Purpose

The Real Estate Management System is a platform that enables real estate owners to publish and manage property advertisements while allowing customers to search, browse, compare, and save properties of interest. The system also provides a Content Management System (CMS) for administrators to manage the platform, its content, property advertisements, customer inquiries, and order-related activities.

---

## 2. Stakeholders

The system consists of the following stakeholder groups:

1. CMS Administrators
    
2. Visitors
    
    - Real Estate Owner
        
    - Customer
        

---

## 3. Stakeholder Responsibilities

### 3.1 CMS Administrator

CMS Administrators are responsible for operating and managing the platform through the administration panel. Their responsibilities include:

- Managing real estate advertisements and their associated property information.
    
- Managing website content.
    
- Managing customer contacts and orders.
    
- Communicating with customers to complete the payment process.
    
- Performing other administrative platform management tasks.
    

---

### 3.2 Real Estate Owner

A Real Estate Owner is a visitor who publishes and manages real estate advertisements.

The Real Estate Owner can:

- Sign in or log in using a phone number and One-Time Password (OTP).
    
- Upload real estate advertisements through the website or mobile application.
    
- Edit advertisements that they own.
    
- Delete advertisements that they own.
    

---

### 3.3 Customer

A Customer is a visitor searching for suitable real estate listings.

The Customer can:

- Sign in or log in using a phone number and One-Time Password (OTP).
    
- Continue using the platform as a guest.
    
- Browse and navigate real estate advertisements to find properties matching their needs.
    
- Add advertisements to their favorites.
    
- Compare advertisements.
    
- Contact administrators to complete the payment process.
    
- Perform all Real Estate Owner functions, including creating, editing, and deleting their own advertisements.
    

---

## 4. High-Level System View

The Real Estate Management System consists of two primary interfaces:

- **CMS Panel:** Used by administrators to manage the platform, real estate advertisements, website content, contacts, orders, and administrative operations.
    
- **Website and Mobile Application:** Used by visitors to browse real estate advertisements, manage their own advertisements, save favorites, compare properties, and communicate with administrators.
    

At the highest level, every user interacting with the Website or Mobile Application is considered a **Visitor**. A visitor may browse as a **Guest**, authenticate using **Phone Number + OTP**, publish and manage their own advertisements, or search for and interact with advertisements created by other visitors.
