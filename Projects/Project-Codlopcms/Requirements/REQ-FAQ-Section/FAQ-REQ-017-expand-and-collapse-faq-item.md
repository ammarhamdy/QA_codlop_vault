## Description
The Mobile App shall allow users to expand and collapse individual FAQ items within the accordion layout to show or hide the corresponding answer.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] The user can expand an FAQ item by interacting with its question.
- [ ] When an FAQ item is expanded, its corresponding answer is displayed.
- [ ] The user can collapse an expanded FAQ item.
- [ ] When an FAQ item is collapsed, its answer is hidden.
- [ ] Expanding or collapsing one FAQ item does not modify its configured question or answer.
- [ ] The expand/collapse behavior applies independently to each FAQ item.
