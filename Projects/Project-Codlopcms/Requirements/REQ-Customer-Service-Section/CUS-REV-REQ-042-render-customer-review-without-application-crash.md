## Description
The Mobile App shall render a valid Customer Review section without causing an application crash when processing its configuration, styles, settings, and customer review content.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] The Mobile App renders a valid Customer Review section without crashing.
- [ ] The Mobile App safely processes the Customer Review section JSON configuration.
- [ ] The Mobile App safely processes the configured Customer Review styles.
- [ ] The Mobile App safely processes the configured Customer Review settings.
- [ ] The Mobile App safely processes the configured customer review items.
- [ ] Invalid or unexpected Customer Review data does not cause the application to crash.
- [ ] A failure in Customer Review rendering does not prevent other valid home screen sections from rendering.
