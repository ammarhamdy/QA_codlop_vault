## Description

The application shall allow users to add products to their shopping cart.

## Acceptance Criteria

-  Users can add a product to the cart from the Product Details screen.
    
-  The application confirms that the product has been added successfully.
    
-  The cart item count is updated after a product is added.
    
-  Added products appear in the Cart screen.
    
-  Adding the same product again increases its quantity instead of creating a duplicate cart item.
    
-  Cart data persists after restarting the application.
