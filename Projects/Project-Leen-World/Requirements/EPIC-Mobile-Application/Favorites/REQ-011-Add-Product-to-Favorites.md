## Description

The application shall allow users to save products to their favorites list for quick access later.

## Acceptance Criteria

-  Users can add a product to favorites from the Product Details screen.
    
-  A visual indication is displayed when a product is marked as favorite.
    
-  Added products appear in the Favorites screen.
    
-  A product cannot appear multiple times in the Favorites list.
    
-  Favorite status persists after restarting the application.
    
-  The application updates the favorite status immediately after the action is performed.
