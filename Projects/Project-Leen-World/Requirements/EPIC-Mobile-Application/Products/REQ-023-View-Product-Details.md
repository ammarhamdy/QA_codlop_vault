## Description

The application shall display detailed information about a selected product retrieved from the Zid API.

## Acceptance Criteria

-  Users can access the Product Details screen by selecting a product from supported application screens.
    
-  The Product Details screen retrieves product information from the Zid API.
    
-  The Product Details screen displays the product name.
    
-  The Product Details screen displays the product images.
    
-  The Product Details screen displays the product price.
    
-  The Product Details screen displays the product description.
    
-  The Product Details screen displays the product availability information, when provided by the API.
    
-  The Product Details screen displays a loading indicator while product information is being retrieved.
    
-  An appropriate error message is displayed when product information cannot be retrieved.
