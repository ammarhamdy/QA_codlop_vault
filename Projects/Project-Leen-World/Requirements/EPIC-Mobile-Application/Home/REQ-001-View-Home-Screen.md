## Description

The application shall display a Home Screen when the user launches the application. The Home Screen shall present content configured through the admin panel and retrieved from the Codlop API, including sliders, categories, and product sections.

## Acceptance Criteria

-  The Home Screen is displayed after the application launches successfully.
    
-  The Home Screen retrieves its content from the Codlop API.
    
-  The Home Screen displays all configured sections returned by the API.
    
-  The Home Screen displays a loading indicator while content is being retrieved.
    
-  The Home Screen displays an appropriate message when content is unavailable.
    
-  The Home Screen supports vertical scrolling when content exceeds the screen height.
    
-  The Home Screen displays the bottom navigation bar.
    
-  The Home Screen refreshes its content when reopened or refreshed by the user.
