### Story Description

**As an Administrator**  
**I want to** manage website settings including Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Website Information, SEO Settings, Contact Information, and Social Media Links  
**So that** I can keep the website content and configuration up to date.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Scenario 1: View Current Settings**  
    **Given** settings are configured in the system  
    **When** I open the General Settings page  
    **Then** all saved settings should be displayed correctly.
    
- **Scenario 2: Update Privacy Policy**  
    **Given** I am on the General Settings page  
    **When** I edit and save the Privacy Policy content  
    **Then** the updated content should be saved and displayed on the website.
    
- **Scenario 3: Update Terms & Conditions**  
    **Given** I am on the General Settings page  
    **When** I edit and save the Terms & Conditions content  
    **Then** the changes should be saved successfully.
    
- **Scenario 4: Update Website Information**  
    **Given** I am on the General Settings page  
    **When** I modify website information and save the changes  
    **Then** the updated information should be stored successfully.
    
- **Scenario 5: Update SEO Settings**  
    **Given** I am on the General Settings page  
    **When** I update SEO fields and save the changes  
    **Then** the SEO configuration should be saved successfully.
    
- **Scenario 6: Update Contact Information**  
    **Given** I am on the General Settings page  
    **When** I update the contact information and save  
    **Then** the changes should be reflected on the website.
    
- **Scenario 7: Update Social Media Links**  
    **Given** I am on the General Settings page  
    **When** I add, edit, or remove social media links and save  
    **Then** the changes should be stored and displayed correctly.
    
- **Scenario 8: Required Fields Validation**  
    **Given** certain settings fields are mandatory  
    **When** I attempt to save without completing them  
    **Then** validation messages should be displayed and saving should be prevented.
    
- **Scenario 9: Successful Save Confirmation**  
    **Given** I update any settings section with valid data  
    **When** the save operation succeeds  
    **Then** the system should display a success message.
