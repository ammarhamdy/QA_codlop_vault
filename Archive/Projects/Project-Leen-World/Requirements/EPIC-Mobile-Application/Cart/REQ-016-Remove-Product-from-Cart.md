## Description

The application shall allow users to remove products from their shopping cart.

## Acceptance Criteria

-  Users can remove a product from the Cart screen.
    
-  Removed products no longer appear in the cart.
    
-  The cart total is recalculated after product removal.
    
-  The cart item count is updated after product removal.
    
-  An empty-state message is displayed when all products have been removed from the cart.
