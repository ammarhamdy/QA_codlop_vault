## Description

The application shall allow users to modify the quantity of products already added to the cart.

## Acceptance Criteria

-  Users can increase the quantity of a cart item.
    
-  Users can decrease the quantity of a cart item.
    
-  The cart total is recalculated after quantity changes.
    
-  The updated quantity is reflected immediately in the Cart screen.
    
-  Quantity changes persist after restarting the application.
