## Description

The application shall allow users to remove products from their favorites list.

## Acceptance Criteria

-  Users can remove a product from the Favorites screen.
    
-  Users can remove a product from the Product Details screen when the product is already marked as favorite.
    
-  Removed products no longer appear in the Favorites screen.
    
-  The favorite indicator is updated immediately after removal.
    
-  The Favorites screen reflects the change without requiring an application restart.
